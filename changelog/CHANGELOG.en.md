# Changelog

## 1.2 – November 6, 2026
- App in German, English, French, Spanish, Portuguese (Portugal) and Polish
- The app follows the phone's language; Android settings let you pick a language per app
- The changelog is shown in German and English

## 1.1 – November 6, 2026
- Extended after the iPhone original: electric, acoustic, classical, rock, muted, electric bass (4 strings) and ukulele
- Effects as pedals with on/off switch: distortion, wah, chorus, delay and reverb
- Delay with time, feedback, level and a TAP button for the tempo
- Tilt effect: tilting the phone raises or lowers the pitch (whammy) or controls the wah, with sensitivity
- Presets like lead guitar, clean chorus, wah solo, electric bass and ukulele
- Custom tuning: raise or lower each string individually
- Switches like on the original: string bending, open hammer-on, pull-off to open note
- Hammer-ons to a higher fret keep the string ringing instead of plucking again
- Portrait mode with a vertical neck and buttons at the bottom like the later iPhone version
- Adjustable string spacing, 3 to 10 visible frets
- Restore default settings

## 1.0 – November 6, 2026
- First version: landscape fretboard with six strings, frets and inlays like the old iPhone original
- Multi-touch: fret notes, strum on the body or swipe across the strings
- Hammer-ons, pull-offs, slides and string bends by moving your finger
- Move the neck up to the 22nd fret with the arrows or "Scroll"
- Acoustic, nylon, clean and rock sounds plus distortion, chorus, delay and reverb
- Microphone tuner with automatic string detection, history trace and reference tone
- Nine tunings (standard, drop D, DADGAD, open G …), chromatic mode and adjustable concert pitch
- Left-handed mode, note names on your fingers, vibration
- What's New and update check in the options
