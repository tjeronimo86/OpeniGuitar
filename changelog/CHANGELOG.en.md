# Changelog

## 1.0 – November 6, 2026
- First version: landscape fretboard with six strings, frets and inlays like the old iPhone original
- Multi-touch: fret notes, strum on the body or swipe across the strings
- Hammer-ons, pull-offs, slides and string bends by moving your finger
- Move the neck up to the 22nd fret with the arrows or "Scroll"
- Acoustic, nylon, clean and rock sounds plus distortion, chorus, delay and reverb
- Microphone tuner with automatic string detection, history trace and reference tone
- Nine tunings (standard, drop D, DADGAD, open G …), chromatic mode and adjustable concert pitch
- Left-handed mode, note names on your fingers, vibration
- What's New and update check in the options
